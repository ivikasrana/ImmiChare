## When the MIT License is enough

The MIT License already allows use, modification and redistribution, including in
commercial products, as long as the copyright notice and license text are kept and
you accept the software "as is" with no warranty.

## When to get a commercial license

Get a commercial license if you need any of the following:

- Use or redistribution **without** the MIT attribution and notice requirement
  (for example white-label or rebranded builds).
- A **warranty**, indemnity or other contractual assurances.
- **Priority support**, bug-fix turnaround or maintenance commitments.
- **Custom features**, integrations or private builds.
- License terms required by your company's procurement or legal policy.

## What a commercial license includes

The exact terms are agreed per customer and may include:

- A perpetual or subscription license for one product, one company or unlimited use.
- Release from the attribution requirement.
- Support and update terms.
- Warranty and liability terms.

## How to get one

Open an issue on this repository titled **"Commercial license"** with your company
name and intended use, and you will be contacted privately with terms and pricing.
